About Us

Marines MC came into existence on November 10, 1994, in El Paso, Texas. The first national run/meeting was at the Four Corners Rally in Ignacio, CO, in September of 1996, followed by one in Angel Fire, NM, in May 1999. Subsequent runs/meetings have convened at Cripple Creek, CO. A national run/meeting is now held every two years at a location designated by and agreed upon by the Membership.

The BloodStripe Chapter of the Marines Motorcycle Club is a proud and recognized chapter committed to honoring the traditions, values, and esprit de corps of the United States Marine Corps. We are a brotherhood built on loyalty, faithfulness, and respect, with a focus on family first, supporting one another, and serving our communities through charity and fellowship. Our chapter welcomes those who are interested, and we invite you to learn more about who we are and what we stand for.
